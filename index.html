Everything you do with a file, one flick away.

Halodial is a dial that opens under your pointer. Drag a file, hold ⌘, and convert, edit, share or file it without opening another app.

This is the real dial, with the options Halodial gives each kind of file. Hover a slot, then click it, or rest on one with options to open it. Click the centre to go back. Nothing here touches your files.

How it works

Three ways in. No windows to open.

Halodial lives in your menu bar and waits. It appears where your pointer already is, and goes away the moment you've chosen.

⌘

Hold ⌘ while dragging a file

The dial opens under the file with File, Convert, Edit and Share. Press the key before or after you start dragging. You can record any keys you like.

Shake a file to park it

A shelf opens beside it. Collect files from different places, then drag them all out together, or act on the whole stack at once.

Tap with four fingers

The launcher: volume, music, display and system controls, your apps and folders. Three fingers if you prefer, or ⌥⌘Space.

Features

Small tools, kept where your hands are.

The shelf

Somewhere to put things down.

Moving files between folders, apps and Spaces usually means juggling windows. Shake the file instead, and come back for it when you're ready.

  • Drop files, links, text and images on it; drag them out one at a time or all together.
  • Name and colour each shelf, keep several open, reopen your last ten.
  • Pin a shelf to the edge of the screen, where it waits as a slim tab.
  • Watch a folder like Downloads, and new files land on a shelf by themselves.

Private by design

Your files never leave your Mac.

Every conversion runs on your Mac, using the encoders built into macOS and into Halodial. There's nothing to sign in to and nothing that watches what you do.

Installing, the first time

  1. Drag Halodial to ApplicationsOpen the downloaded file and drag the Halodial icon onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open it oncemacOS says it can't check Halodial for malicious software, because Halodial isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done.
  3. Choose Open AnywayIn System Settings, open Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Halodial, then confirm. You only do this once.
  4. Say helloA short welcome shows the three ways in. After that Halodial waits in the menu bar, and updates arrive by themselves.
